Who We Are



Deriam and Dahiana Vasil are a couple and the founders of this project. Born with a deep passion for the ocean and sport navigation, they decided to combine their experience to create the premier boat rental and fishing class service in Clearwater.

We are a team of ocean enthusiasts, local fishermen, and certified guides dedicated to connecting people with the sea. With years of experience navigating our coasts, we combine a deep respect for marine life with the thrill of sport fishing.

Purpose

To facilitate safe, responsible, and exciting access to the ocean, creating memorable experiences on the water and fostering a community of passionate boaters and anglers.

Company mission

To inspire and equip the next generation of ocean lovers through education, premium boat rentals, and sustainable fishing charters that exceed our clients' expectations.
